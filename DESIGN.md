# Design Brief

## Direction

DWELL Homes & Services — Premium trust-first real estate advisory for Pune, positioned as modern consultancy, not traditional broker.

## Tone

Minimal luxury with glassmorphism — refined, warm, professional, human. Sophisticated yet approachable. Sophisticated yet approachable.

## Differentiation

Electric teal primary (#009FD4) + champagne gold accents on soft white create premium trust perception. Glassmorphism cards and airy spacing signal modern advisory intelligence.

## Color Palette

| Token      | OKLCH           | Role              |
|------------|-----------------|-------------------|
| background | 0.98 0.008 230  | soft white base   |
| foreground | 0.18 0.015 230  | charcoal text     |
| card       | 1.0 0.004 230   | pure white cards  |
| primary    | 0.55 0.18 240   | electric teal     |
| accent     | 0.72 0.14 70    | champagne gold    |
| muted      | 0.94 0.01 230   | light gray        |
| success    | 0.6 0.16 150    | emerald green     |

## Typography

- Display: Space Grotesk — bold headlines, CTAs, section titles
- Body: General Sans — readable, modern, premium
- Scale: hero 56px, h2 40px, h3 28px, body 16px, label 14px

## Elevation & Depth

Glassmorphism cards with soft shadows create layered depth. Soft white backgrounds with pure white cards and subtle shadows (8-25px blur) for elevation.

## Structural Zones

| Zone    | Background      | Border                  | Notes                          |
|---------|-----------------|-------------------------|--------------------------------|
| Header  | glass blur      | gold/teal accent line   | sticky, premium navbar         |
| Content | soft white      | none                    | alternating card zones         |
| Footer  | light gray      | subtle top border       | professional trust statement   |

## Spacing & Rhythm

Airy spacing: 48px section gaps, 32px card padding, 16px element gaps. Large typography creates hierarchy. Mobile-first breakpoints sm/md/lg for responsive rhythm.

## Component Patterns

- Buttons: gold/teal accent, 8px radius, gradient fills, hover lift
- Cards: pure white, 8px radius, glass backdrop, subtle shadow
- Badges: trust indicators with emerald green, minimal icon treatment

## Motion

- Entrance: fade-in-up 0.6s on scroll, staggered children
- Hover: card-lift with shadow glow (0.3s), button shine effect
- Decorative: floating badges, shimmer accent lines

## Constraints

- No dark mode (light premium aesthetic)
- No harsh shadows or neon glows
- Glass effects use white overlay + blur, not black
- Gold accents secondary to teal primary

## Signature Detail

Glassmorphism cards with soft teal glow on hover — signals premium modern advisory while maintaining trust-first minimalism.
